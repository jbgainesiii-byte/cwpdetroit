What we actually do

The rooms that make a home.

From the kitchen sink to fresh bedroom floors, we build a checklist around the home. Explore what a typical clean includes; your walkthrough confirms the details.

KitchenWhere the day starts and the mail piles up
  • Counters and backsplash wiped down
  • Sink scrubbed and shined
  • Stovetop and range cleaned
  • Outside of fridge, oven, and dishwasher
  • Microwave cleaned inside and out
  • Table and chairs wiped
  • Floors swept and mopped
  • Trash taken out
BathroomsThe room families worry about most
  • Toilet cleaned and sanitized
  • Tub and shower scrubbed
  • Sink, counter, and faucet
  • Mirror polished
  • Fixtures and handles shined
  • Floors washed
  • Trash emptied
  • Fresh towels set out, if left for us
BedroomsWhere the day winds down
  • Beds made (linens changed when left out)
  • Nightstands and surfaces dusted
  • Mirrors and glass
  • A gentle tidy and reset
  • Floors vacuumed or mopped
  • Trash emptied
Living & family roomsWhere the visits and the grandkids happen
  • Dusting, including reachable high-touch spots
  • Surfaces and side tables
  • Cushions and throws straightened
  • Glass tables and TV screens
  • Floors vacuumed or mopped
  • A light tidy, nothing moved without asking
Whole-home touchesThe little things that add up
  • Light switches, door handles, and railings
  • Baseboards (on a rotating schedule)
  • Interior glass and entryways
  • A supply check for what’s running low
On requestJust ask at the walkthrough
  • Inside the fridge
  • Inside the oven
  • Interior windows
  • Laundry washed or folded
  • Light decluttering, only with permission
  • Seasonal deep-clean focus areas

Tailored to the home. We agree on the checklist at the walkthrough and summarize completed work in your visit update. Heavy furniture moving, high-ladder work, and personal or medical care are outside our cleaning service.

What we do, and what we never do.

Cleaning With Purpose is a home cleaning and family communication service. It is not home health care, and you should hear that from us, plainly.

We always

  • Clean the home with care and consistency
  • Send the agreed visit update to your chosen contact
  • Mention practical things we notice, like supplies running low
  • Respect the resident’s preferences and permission

We never

  • Provide medical care or health advice
  • Handle or remind about medication
  • Help with bathing, dressing, or mobility
  • Monitor health or wellness
  • Act as an emergency response service

Personal or medical care should be arranged separately with an appropriate provider. If we encounter an immediate emergency during a visit, we call 911 and then the designated contact.

Questions families ask

Asked and answered.

Is this medical or personal care?
We provide home cleaning and agreed updates about the work. We do not provide personal care, medication support, health monitoring, or medical advice. Those services should be arranged separately with an appropriate provider.
Who decides what gets shared with family?
We agree with the resident, or their authorized representative where appropriate, on the cleaning priorities and who receives updates. The Purpose Note covers the home and the work completed. Photos require written permission; updates are never a secret from the person whose home it is.
What if my mom says she doesn’t need help?
The walkthrough is a chance to meet Danielle, ask questions, and decide what would actually help. Your parent can set the priorities and decide whether to go ahead. There is no obligation to book a clean.
How do you handle keys, codes, and cameras?
With a written access policy set before we arrive: keys, lockboxes, codes, parking, pets, all of it. Cameras in the home are completely fine with us.
Why does the first visit cost more?
The Purpose Home Reset is deeper work: it brings the home back to comfortable and teaches us its priorities. That’s what lets us quote your recurring plan honestly. It’s a reset, not a discounted trial.
What areas do you serve?
We serve Metro Detroit and the surrounding communities: Detroit, Dearborn, Royal Oak, Ferndale, Southfield, Troy, Warren, Grosse Pointe, and Livonia. Share the home’s city or ZIP with your request so we can confirm availability.
How do I get an exact price?
Book the free walkthrough below: thirty minutes at the house. You’ll have a clear plan and an exact quote in writing before anything is booked.
