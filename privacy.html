Privacy notice

Updated September 6, 2026

This notice covers this website and inquiries about Cleaning With Purpose home cleaning services.

Information you choose to share

When you call, text, or email, you may share your name, contact details, the home’s city, and cleaning preferences. We use those details to respond and arrange the walkthrough. Your email or phone provider also handles the message you send.

If an online request form is available, its required and optional fields are labeled. Online requests and any optional photos are processed through Netlify Forms. Please send only photos you have permission to share, without people or personal documents. Do not send medical records, access codes, or payment information through a website inquiry.

Information from visiting the site

This website does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or browser storage. The hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and requested page to deliver and protect the site. The current public site is hosted by GitHub Pages; a deployment on Netlify is handled by Netlify.

Some pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. That sends a request to Google with technical information needed to return the font. See the Google Fonts privacy explanation, GitHub privacy statement, and Netlify privacy statement for those providers’ practices.

Updates about someone’s home

Cleaning priorities and recipients of visit updates are agreed with the resident, or an appropriate authorized representative. Photos for visit updates require written permission. The Purpose Note describes the home and cleaning work; it is not a health assessment.

Questions about your information

To ask about information you have shared, including a request to correct or remove it, contact Danielle at info@cleaningwithpurpose.org or 313-451-2221. Please describe your request without sending sensitive documents.

Need help using the website?

If a page or form is difficult to use, call or text 313-451-2221. You can arrange a walkthrough by phone.
